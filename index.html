graphic design, editorial print + web & experimental — france

after completing a bachelor's degree in editorial & transmedia graphic design in toulouse, i am pursuing my master's degree in graphic design & visual languages @ la hear in strasbourg.

i would define my creative process as experimental, exploring different forms and mixing them to create new ones. growing up with the internet, has shaped my visual universe, particularly through cybercultures. queer subcultures and diy culture are also major influences in my work. my practice moves between analog and digital, combining graphic design, moving images, interactive media and performance.

i see graphic design not only as a tool for communication, but also as a way to question, explore and express ideas.

01. glitter riot

editorial + thesis

2026

this research project explores hyperfemininity as a visual language and a form of resistance
for marginalized communities. through kitsch, maximalism and excess, i look at how aesthetics
often seen as superficial can challenge gender norms and become tools for self-expression, visibility and community building. inspired by queer and online subcultures, this project explores graphic design as a critical and sensitive practice, at the intersection of editorial design, queer culture
and diy practices.

02. baragouine

editorial

since 2024

baragouine is an independant lesbian magazine that first issued in october 2023.
this project aim to create a tangible archive of lesbian experiences. the magazine is made as a collective and also publishes contribution from external writers and artists that reflects around a new theme at each new issue.
more on @baragouinex and www.helloasso/baragouine

06. dracula

editorial + binding

2025

an editorial reinterpretation of bram stoker’s dracula, exploring how typography can become part of a book’s narrative. across 680 pages, expressive typography and a double printing process combine laser-printed text with inkjet graphic effects. a pink halo gradually appears around characters who are vampires or are undergoing a transformation, growing stronger as their identities are revealed. here the colour becomes a playful visual code for the presence of vampirism.
